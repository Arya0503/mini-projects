Our Story

Founded in 1986, Greenwood Public Library began as a small collection of donated books in a repurposed warehouse. Over the decades, it has transformed into a modern architectural landmark, serving thousands of residents daily. Our commitment remains the same: democratizing access to information.

We have constantly evolved to meet the needs of the digital age. From offering high-speed internet and digital media labs to hosting thousands of e-books, we ensure our patrons are equipped for the future. The library is a living entity, growing alongside the minds that walk through our doors.
